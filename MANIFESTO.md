# UNSOLVED Manifesto

Institutions run on status fields. People live in physical reality.

Those two worlds do not always agree.

A ticket can say **closed** while the light is still dark. A work order can say **complete** while the hazard is still there. A dashboard can turn green before the street does.

UNSOLVED exists for that gap.

We believe public accountability should be:

- **specific** — one documented condition, not vague outrage;
- **precommitted** — define what `done` means before the outcome is known;
- **evidence-led** — preserve the record and let the proof carry the conclusion;
- **independently verifiable** — another reviewer should be able to reproduce the result;
- **transparent** — scoring and state rules should be inspectable and versioned;
- **fair** — paying customers and public critics face the same verification standard;
- **privacy-aware** — public truth does not require public exposure of every raw file;
- **correctable** — factual mistakes should be fixed without erasing the history of the correction.

UNSOLVED is not built to manufacture outrage. It is built to manufacture clarity.

The protocol makes a deliberately narrow promise:

> **We will distinguish what was officially claimed from what the evidence can actually verify.**

Everything else grows from there.
