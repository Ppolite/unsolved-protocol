# Public Protocol / Commercial Boundary

UNSOLVED is designed so public trust does not depend on exposing private operational systems.

## Public layer

This repository may publish:

- challenge and resolution-state definitions;
- Accountability Score methodology;
- evidence admissibility and provenance standards;
- governance and correction rules;
- public schemas;
- pilot methodology;
- synthetic examples;
- changelogs and methodology version history.

These materials exist to make public conclusions inspectable and reproducible.

## Private commercial layer

The operating company may keep proprietary:

- authentication and account infrastructure;
- billing and entitlement systems;
- institutional workflow software;
- commercial API implementation;
- customer-specific integrations;
- fraud and abuse detection;
- anti-gaming heuristics;
- verifier reputation models;
- private moderation systems;
- sensitive evidence storage;
- infrastructure configuration and secrets;
- internal risk scoring and operational tooling.

## Revenue stack

### UNSOLVED Public
Free public challenge discovery, evidence participation, escalation tracking, and institutional profiles.

Phase 1 uses pledge-backed targets with zero platform fee and no UNSOLVED custody of challenge funds.

### UNSOLVED Pro
Target price: **$99/month**.

Audience: journalists, researchers, neighborhood organizations, and civic analysts.

Potential features:
- advanced alerts;
- historical comparisons;
- evidence reports;
- data exports;
- saved institutional watchlists.

### UNSOLVED Institutional
Target range: **$999–$4,999+/month**, depending on organization size and workflow requirements.

Potential features:
- official response console;
- case claiming and assignment;
- internal routing;
- verification submissions;
- backlog analytics;
- resolution-performance benchmarking;
- team and audit controls.

Payment must never purchase score suppression or favorable public classification.

### UNSOLVED Intelligence API
Usage-based commercial access to structured resolution and institutional-performance data.

Potential query families:
- institution profile and score;
- challenge resolution state;
- claimed-versus-verified closure;
- historical resolution latency;
- responsibility history;
- geographic failure trends;
- evidence confidence metadata.

### Verification & sponsored programs
UNSOLVED may charge for coordinating qualified third-party verification and for sponsored challenge programs, while keeping designated repair funds separate from platform revenue.

### Future predictive intelligence
Once the dataset is sufficiently large and representative, UNSOLVED may offer probabilistic estimates of resolution time, delay risk, or institutional responsiveness. Predictive products must clearly distinguish forecast from observed fact.

## Principle

**The rules remain public. Operating the verification network does not have to be free.**
