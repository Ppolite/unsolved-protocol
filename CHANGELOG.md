# Changelog

All material changes to the public UNSOLVED protocol are recorded here.

## 0.1-draft — 2026-09-04

Initial public protocol package.

### Added

- challenge and Resolution Contract model;
- separate resolution and responsibility states;
- Accountability Score weighting and confidence rules;
- evidence provenance and privacy standard;
- governance, appeals, and commercial-independence principles;
- public/commercial boundary and initial revenue stack;
- Phase 1 pilot design and pass/fail criteria;
- machine-readable challenge and resolution-event schemas;
- repository notice and early contribution policy;
- protocol manifesto.

### Pilot posture

Phase 1 is pre-launch and uses pledge-backed targets only. No production scoring claims should be inferred from the draft methodology until real pilot data has been evaluated.
